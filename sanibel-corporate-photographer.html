Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know
Before Booking

How quickly can you turn around corporate headshots?
Standard delivery is 48 hours for headshots and 48–72 hours for events. Rush delivery (same day or next morning) is available — inquire when booking. We know your LinkedIn profile and website can't wait a week.
Can you come to our office in Sanibel Island?
Yes — on-location is our most popular option. We bring all equipment including portable lighting, backdrops if needed, and handle everything on-site. We work around your team's schedule and can photograph individuals throughout the day between meetings.
Do we receive commercial use rights to the photos?
Yes. All corporate packages include full commercial licensing — your company can use the images on your website, LinkedIn, press releases, annual reports, and marketing materials without additional licensing fees.
How many photos will we receive?
For headshot sessions: 5–10 fully edited images per person (3 guaranteed selects minimum). For event coverage: typically 100–200+ edited images for a 4-hour event. We don't charge per image — you get everything usable, fully edited.
Can you photograph a conference or multi-day event in Sanibel Island?
Absolutely. We handle multi-day corporate events regularly across Lee County. Day rates are available. We'll coordinate with your events team, brief on the schedule, and deliver a complete organized gallery by service — keynote, breakout sessions, networking, awards, etc.
