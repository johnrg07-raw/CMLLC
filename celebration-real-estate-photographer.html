Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know
Before Booking

How fast is delivery?
Standard delivery is 24–48 hours after the shoot — MLS-ready, edited, and color-corrected. Same-day rush delivery is available for an additional $150, perfect for listings that need to go live immediately.
Do you photograph for homeowners and Airbnb hosts?
Yes — realtors get MLS-ready imagery, homeowners get listing-grade photos for FSBO or pre-listing, and Airbnb / short-term rental hosts get curated galleries optimized for booking conversion. We adapt the deliverables to the platform.
What is included in the drone aerial coverage?
Drone coverage includes 8–15 high-resolution aerial stills showing the property, surrounding neighborhood, and key amenities. For waterfront, golf course, or unique-lot listings, drone aerials dramatically improve buyer engagement and click-through rates.
Can you produce a video walkthrough?
Yes — Listing Pro includes a 60–90 second walkthrough, Luxury Listing includes a 2–3 minute cinematic tour. Walkthroughs are color-graded, stabilized, and delivered MP4-ready for MLS, Zillow, Realtor.com, social media, and Airbnb.
Do you do twilight shoots?
Yes — twilight exterior shoots are included in the Luxury Listing package and available as an add-on for $125 on other packages. Twilight imagery boosts listing engagement by 30–40%, especially for luxury and waterfront properties.
Do you serve other Central Florida cities besides Celebration?
Yes — we cover the Greater Orlando market including Kissimmee, Champions Gate, Davenport, Lake Buena Vista, and Reunion and beyond. There are no hidden trip fees for listings within the metro area.
What file formats do we receive?
All photos delivered as high-resolution JPGs (web-optimized + print-ready), and videos as MP4 (1080p standard, 4K available on Luxury Listing). Files arrive via private Pixieset link with download buttons — easy to share with your MLS provider or upload directly to Zillow, Realtor.com, Airbnb, and VRBO.
