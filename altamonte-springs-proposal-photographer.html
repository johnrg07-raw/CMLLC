Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know
Before Booking

How far in advance should I book a proposal photographer in Altamonte Springs?
For most proposal sessions, 2–4 weeks is ideal. For full setup packages (floral arch, rose petals, live violinist), we need at least 4 weeks to coordinate vendors. Spring weekends in Altamonte Springs book quickly — don't wait if you have a date in mind.
Will my partner know you're there?
No. We use telephoto zoom lenses (70–200mm) from 50–100 feet away. We arrive early, scout the position, dress casually, and never make eye contact. In 50+ proposals, we've never been spotted.
What's the best time of day to propose in Altamonte Springs?
Golden hour — 60 minutes before sunset — gives the warmest, most cinematic light. For waterfront and beach locations in Altamonte Springs, sunrise can be equally magical. We'll give you a precise window based on your chosen date and location.
How do I get my proposal photos?
A sneak peek of 20–30 images arrives in your private Pixieset gallery within 24 hours. The full edited gallery — every photo, full resolution — is delivered within 48–72 hours. Video packages: 5–7 business days. Gallery stays active for 1 year.
What if the weather is bad?
For severe weather we reschedule at no charge. Light rain creates beautiful moody photos — some of our most dramatic shots came from overcast sessions. We'll assess conditions together and make the call.
