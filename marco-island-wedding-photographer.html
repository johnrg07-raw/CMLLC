Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know
Before Booking

How far in advance should I book my Marco Island wedding photographer?
For peak season (October–April) we recommend booking 6–12 months in advance. Summer and weekday weddings have more availability. Popular Marco Island dates go fast — once your date is set, reach out immediately.
Is a second photographer included?
Yes — a second photographer is included in The Forever (full-day) package at no extra cost. For the Half Day package, a second shooter can be added for $300. Two photographers means we capture both partners getting ready simultaneously, and catch every angle during the ceremony.
When will we receive our wedding photos?
Your full edited gallery is delivered within 2–3 weeks via private Pixieset link with full-resolution downloads. A teaser of 20–30 images arrives within 48 hours of your wedding.
Do you travel for destination weddings outside of Marco Island?
Absolutely. We've photographed weddings across Florida, New York, Punta Cana, and internationally. The Away Wedding package quotes everything as a single custom number — no surprises.
What is your backup plan for equipment failure?
We carry redundant camera bodies, multiple lenses, and backup memory cards. Every image is written to two cards simultaneously. We've photographed 100+ events without a single equipment failure.
What Marco Island venues do you work at?
We've photographed weddings at the JW Marriott Marco Island, the Hilton Marco Island Beach Resort, the Marco Beach Ocean Resort, the Marco Island Yacht Club, Esplanade Shoppes, and the Hideaway Beach Club, and dozens of private venues, country clubs, and waterfront estates throughout Marco Island and Collier County.
