Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know
Before Booking

How fast is delivery?
Standard delivery is 24–48 hours after the shoot — edited, color-graded, and platform-ready. Same-day rush is available for $100.
Who do you fly for?
Real estate agents and brokers, homeowners pre-listing, Airbnb/STR hosts, wedding venues, event planners, construction firms tracking progress, and brand campaigns. We adapt deliverables to each platform.
What about FAA airspace restrictions?
We check airspace before every shoot. For restricted areas near airports we file authorization requests in advance. Central Florida has Class B/D airspace around MCO and Orlando Executive that we navigate routinely.
Can you do aerial video?
Yes — Aerial Photo + Video and Aerial Production packages include 4K cinematic video. Smooth motion, color graded, MP4 delivery for listings, social media, or marketing.
What aircraft do you fly?
DJI prosumer aircraft (Mavic 3 / Air 3 class) capable of 4K video at 60fps and 48-megapixel stills. Backup batteries and props on every shoot.
Do you fly outside of Deltona?
Yes — we cover Volusia County and the Greater Orlando metro including DeBary, Orange City, Lake Helen, Enterprise, and Sanford. No hidden trip fees within the service area.
